When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Placentia

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Take pictures of damaged materials prior to removal, since the insurer may want them later.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
